# Reactions

Raft's 7 quick reactions, in this order: `thumbs_up.svg`, `heart.svg`, `party_popper.svg`, `eyes.svg`, `fire.svg`, `joy.svg` and `check_mark.svg`. They come from the product's own set (`packages/web/assets/reactions` in the Raft web app), with the same subjects, order, shapes and colors ([Brand](../../foundations/brand.md#reactions)):

- Every color but the check mark's comes from the palette. The check mark has two greens of its own and a cream check, as in the product. The confetti adds pink and cyan.
- Highlights are white square pixels instead of the product's cream curves: every dot is a pixel.
- They are drawn on a 24px grid with a 2px line, in a darker shade of each drawing's main color instead of ink, so the same files read on the light and the dark canvas: the outline draws the edge in light mode, the fill in dark mode. They get no die-cut edge in dark mode.

`beers.svg` is drawn too, but it is not a quick reaction. It uses a 1.5px line, since its two mugs are smaller shapes.

The rules for their sizes, dark mode and accessibility are in [Brand](../../foundations/brand.md#reactions).
